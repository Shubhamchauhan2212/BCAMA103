About Cricket

Cricket is the most loved outdoor game in India, enjoyed both nationally and internationally. It's a passionate sport played with a bat and a ball. Two teams, each with 11 players, compete to score the most runs. The aim is to win by achieving a higher score than the opposing team.

About foot ball

Football is a well-known sport all around the world. The game of football develops in the players a fair mind, team effort, and competitive spirit. It is regarded as one of the most disciplined sports in the world. Football is quite popular in all countries.

About kho kho

Kho Kho is a traditional sport in India. Children play this game from an early age as a recreational tag game. Older people also have a similar attraction to it. Like Kabaddi, Kho Kho is an outdoor sport and is considered to be a game of tactics and strategy.
